# 📊 Customer Segmentation & Sales Performance Dashboard using Microsoft Excel

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **Customer Segmentation & Sales Performance Dashboard** developed in **Microsoft Excel**. It analyzes retail sales data to uncover customer behavior, product performance, regional sales trends, and business profitability.

The dashboard enables users to monitor key performance indicators (KPIs), explore sales trends, and make data-driven business decisions using Excel's advanced analytics capabilities.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze overall sales performance
- Understand customer segmentation
- Identify top-performing products
- Compare sales across regions
- Evaluate profit by product category
- Analyze payment methods
- Track monthly sales trends
- Support business decision-making with interactive dashboards

## 💡 Business Insights

- Consumer customers contribute the highest sales.
- Technology products generate the highest revenue.
- Corporate customers produce strong profitability.
- Sales show an increasing monthly trend.
- Credit Card is the most preferred payment method.
- Western and Eastern regions contribute significantly to overall revenue.
